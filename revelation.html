History of the Faith · Path One

Revelation

The covenant of grace is one covenant, revealed progressively through Scripture — by farther steps, promise upon promise — until its full disclosure in the New Testament. This page will trace that unfolding.

The Covenant Framework
  • Covenant of RedemptionThe eternal agreement between the Father and the Son to accomplish the redemption of a people.
  • Covenant of WorksGod's covenant with Adam in the garden, promising life on condition of perfect obedience.
  • Covenant of GraceGod's covenant to save sinners through faith in Christ, revealed progressively after the fall.
Defining a Covenant

An agreement between two or more parties and terms which lead to defined blessings or curses.

Classifying Covenants

Covenants can be understood along two axes: who the covenant is made with, and what its terms are.

By Parties
  • Particular — made with a specific people or group.
  • Creation — made with the whole of creation.
By Terms
  • Works — “Do this and live.”
  • Grace — “Live and do this.” There is only one covenant of grace.
Parties Particular Covenants Made with a specific people or group, not all of creation. Parties Covenants with Creation Made with the whole of creation, not a particular people.
