A history of the faith

Particular History

In his wisdom, God has chosen to reveal the good news of the gospel to us through history. This site seeks to give credence to history both in terms of how God revealed the gospel to his people, and how his people grew in their understanding of his revelation over time. In short, I'd like to use this site to account for the history of the faith as it leads to the one particular expression that I believe to be most faithful and most helpful — that of the Particular Baptist. Explore below to see the history of the revelation of the gospel through covenants, and the history of the church's understanding of God and the scriptures through confessions and creeds. My prayer is that this site blesses you and deepens your love for God, his scriptures, and his people the church.
