Who is Particular History for?

Particular History

If you’ve ever wondered how the Old Testament connects to the New Testament, or how the history of Christianity connects to the faith confessed today, this is the place to explore those questions through Scripture, historical sources, and a Reformed Baptist perspective. The two paths below trace the history of the Christian faith toward its expression in the Second London Baptist Confession of 1689.

Path One

History of the Faith: Revelation

How the covenant of grace was revealed progressively through Scripture, by farther steps, from Eden to the New Covenant.
Path Two

History of the Faith: The Church

How the church confessed and defended that revelation across the centuries — from Nicaea to the Second London Confession.
