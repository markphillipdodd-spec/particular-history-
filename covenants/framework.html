The theological framework

The Covenant Framework

Three covenants organize how Scripture's covenants are understood theologically: an eternal covenant among the persons of the Trinity, and two historical covenants — works and grace — through which God relates to mankind.

Covenant of Redemption
The eternal agreement between the Father and the Son to accomplish the redemption of a people.
