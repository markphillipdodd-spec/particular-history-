A.D. 1658 · extended material

Savoy Declaration

Can a shared Reformed doctrine coexist with real disagreement over church government?

Historical Context
English Congregationalists, freed from state-church constraints during the Interregnum, wanted a confession of their own.
Controversy
The dispute was over Presbyterian versus Congregationalist church polity, not over the underlying covenant theology both sides held.
Confession / Clarification
Adapted Westminster, revising chiefly the sections on church government while retaining its covenant theology almost entirely intact.
Downstream Influence
Shows federal theology was portable across polity disagreements — the doctrine, not the church order, was the shared inheritance being carried forward.
