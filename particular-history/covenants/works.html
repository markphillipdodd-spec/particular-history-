Classified by terms

Covenants of Works

“Do this and live.”

The Basic Shape
A covenant of works conditions blessing on obedience rendered. The reward follows the doing; life is held out as the result of a righteousness the covenant party must produce. This is a question of terms — a covenant of works may still be made with a particular people or with creation as a whole, since parties and terms are two separate axes.
