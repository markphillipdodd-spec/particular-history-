The Covenant of Grace

“Live and do this.”

The Basic Shape
A covenant of grace reverses the order: life and standing are given first, as a gift, and obedience flows from what has already been graciously granted. Scripture speaks of one covenant of grace, revealed progressively across many administrations rather than as separate covenants. This is a question of terms, not parties — the one covenant of grace is administered to a particular people across history.
