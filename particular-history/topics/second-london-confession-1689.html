A.D. 1689 · extended material

Second London Confession

Who rightly constitutes the visible covenant community, and what is the proper sign of belonging to it?

Historical Context
Particular Baptists sought public doctrinal clarity and legitimacy in the years surrounding England's 1689 Toleration Act.
Controversy
Paedobaptist federal theology (Westminster, Savoy) extended the covenant sign to believers' infant children; Baptists disputed this specific application of the covenant.
Confession / Clarification
Adapted Westminster and Savoy almost word for word on God, Christ, and the covenant of grace, but restricted baptism to professed believers only.
Downstream Influence
The thread's terminus for now: the same trinitarian, Christological, and covenantal skeleton inherited from Nicaea through Westminster, refined on the question of covenant membership.
