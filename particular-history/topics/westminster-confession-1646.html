A.D. 1646 · extended material

Westminster Confession

How does all of redemptive history — creation, fall, redemption — cohere as a single unified structure?

Historical Context
An English Civil War-era assembly, commissioned by Parliament, set out to reform the Church of England's doctrine.
Controversy
Competing Anglican, Presbyterian, and Independent visions of doctrine and church government all needed reconciling into one confession.
Confession / Clarification
Organizes redemptive history around a covenant of works with Adam and a covenant of grace fulfilled in Christ — full federal theology.
Downstream Influence
Federalism becomes the organizing key for the whole biblical story; Adam's and Christ's covenant headships are set as deliberate mirrors of one another.
