A.D. 1618 · extended material

Canons of Dort

Is salvation ultimately God's unilateral, gracious act, or a cooperative work involving human choice as a determining condition?

Historical Context
The Dutch Reformed church convened an international synod to address a growing rift inside its own theological house.
Controversy
The Arminian Remonstrance challenged unconditional election and irresistible grace, proposing a more conditional, synergistic account of salvation.
Confession / Clarification
Dort articulated what later became known as the five points: total depravity, unconditional election, particular redemption, irresistible grace, perseverance of the saints.
Downstream Influence
Sharpens the covenant of grace as unilateral and gracious, feeding directly into the fuller federal architecture Westminster builds a generation later.
