A.D. 1561 · extended material

Belgic Confession

How does the church confess its faith formally under threat, and how does God relate to his people across history?

Historical Context
Guido de Brès wrote for Reformed believers facing persecution in the Low Countries under Spanish Catholic rule.
Controversy
State-church authorities treated Reformed teaching as heretical sedition, forcing believers to state their faith publicly at real risk to themselves.
Confession / Clarification
Grounds faith in Scripture's sufficient authority over councils, popes, and tradition, and begins to formalize a covenantal reading of redemptive history.
Downstream Influence
The thread visibly bends toward federal theology here — the seed of everything that becomes fuller covenant theology by Westminster and 1689.
